## Beats (frame-level, from 5 fps sequence sheets)

### Belt / shirt slit (4.6 - 7.6 s), `sheets/seq_belt_4.6.jpg`
- 4.6-5.0 s: four buttons visible, no gap. Belt is straight.
- 5.2 s: a hair-thin dark slit forms between two buttons; a spark of orange at its lower end.
- 5.4 s: three small blue crescent strokes appear on each side of the upper button (tension lines), buckle tongue jerks.
- 5.6-7.0 s: slit widens in pulses (thin - wide - thin - wider), strokes flicker on and off, belt bends with the belly
  and the loops shift, the tongue jiggles about every 0.8 s.
- 7.0-7.6 s: slit maximum (about 8 % of the frame width), ember glows warm at its lower end, then the dive.

### Flame and pointing hands (7.6 - 10.4 s), `sheets/seq_fingers_7.6.jpg`
- 7.6 s: slit with a small flame inside.
- 7.8 s: full-frame blur, concentric organic ovals (cave layers) rush toward the camera.
- 8.0 s: cave focus lands; flame worried (eyes up, tiny mouth).
- 8.2 s: first hands slide in from left and right; by 8.6 s the full ring of 8 pointing hands is in place.
- 8.6-10.4 s: hands bob, flame sparks, extremely slow push-in. Cut to the man at 10.4.

### Keys and jars (36.5 - 39.5 s), `sheets/seq_jars_36.5.jpg`
- 36.5-37.5: buildings glide out left, keys (gold, ring head, step-teeth) stream in an arc from lower left to upper right.
- 37.7-38.1: stream ends, jars slide in from right with a pan.
- 38.1-39.5: a key enters the padlock of the big jar (key drifts in from left, ring turns), padlock opens with a tiny
  hop, lid pops (about 6 frames to leave, spinning).

### Birthday (0 - 2.1 s), `sheets/seq_intro_0.jpg`
- 0.0 s eyes open, glaring; 0.2 s wide; 0.4 s eyes squeezed shut + "o" mouth; 0.6 s flames lean + smoke wisps;
  0.8-1.0 s all out, whole scene turns dim blue; 1.2 s three-four candles reignite; 1.4 s all back;
  1.6-2.0 s eyebrows flat, half-lid, eyes close in resignation. Cut.

## Reuse checklist for a NEW short built on this rhythm
1. Open on a character-driven gag (2 s), then a 2-3 s montage of 3 quick hits showing time passing.
2. Hit the "problem" in a clean macro hold (3 s) with a slow drift and a growing physical symptom.
3. Dive through a hole into the "inside" world. Introduce the hero (small glowing thing with a face) as judged/doubted.
4. Spend 25-30 s in the paper-diorama mechanism: 4-6 holds of 3-8 s each, one metaphor per hold, each ending in a
   light-up payoff.
5. Insert one comic cutaway in a different (flat, bright) look for contrast.
6. End on the hero, happy, with one new tiny character entering.
